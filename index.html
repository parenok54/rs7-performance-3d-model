01 — Silhouette

A coupé with five doors

5,009 mm long, with a roofline that flows smoothly into the tail. At 100 km/h a spoiler extends from the boot lid.

02 — Inside

What's under the body

The car comes apart at the seams: doors, bonnet, roof, bumpers. Inside you can see what lies beneath. The turbos sit right inside the V of the engine (a “hot V” layout). An eight-speed tiptronic, quattro all-wheel drive and a 48 V mild hybrid.
